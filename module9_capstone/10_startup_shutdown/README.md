## The situation (read this before you open the worksheet)

D-201 is about to be started up after a scheduled maintenance window. Separately, it tripped once before on high bottoms temperature, which raises the obvious question of whether it can simply be restarted once cooled. The startup and shutdown sequence in the SOP has a specific order for a reason; your agent's job is to make sure nobody skips a step, especially under the pressure of getting production back online quickly.
